Privacy Policy for Super Busy
Last Updated: 28 August 2026

We respect your privacy; this game is designed to collect as little information as possible.

1. Personal Information We Do Not Collect
Because you download and play Super Busy directly on your device without signing in, we do not collect any personal data.

 No Personal Data: No names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
 No Accounts: No account creation or login details required.
 No Wallet Tracking: The game does not request a wallet connection (e.g., “Wallet Connect”) nor interact with your device’s Seed Vault. We do not view, collect, or log your public wallet addresses or transaction history.
 No Messaging: No player text or chat inputs are collected.

2. Information We May Collect: Technical Data & Analytics (Bug Fixing)
To help us identify bugs, prevent crashes, and improve gameplay, we may use basic analytics tools. These tools do not personally identify you; they only examine anonymous technical data from your device, which may include:
 Device type and operating system version.
 Game performance metrics (such as frame rates or loading times).
 Anonymous crash logs and error reports.

3. Third-Party Services
If we use third-party analytics platforms (such as Unity, Firebase, or Google Analytics), they handle your device’s technical data according to their own privacy policies. These services do not share your personal identity with us.

4. Children’s Privacy
Super Busy is suitable for all ages. Since we do not collect, store, or share any personal information, we fully comply with children’s privacy regulations, including COPPA and GDPR.

5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy when the game is updated. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last Updated” date.
